How it works

Three steps, all inside the Messages app you already use.

  1. 1

    Before you go

    Plan

    Text Batman before a first date, a Marketplace pickup, or an apartment viewing. It asks a couple of quick questions.

  2. 2

    While you're there

    Quiet watch

    Share your location for just that window. Batman stays silent while things look normal.

  3. 3

    Only if something's off

    Escalate

    If something looks off it checks in. Go quiet and it alerts the friend you chose with your last location.

Two code words, for when you can't say it out loud

You pick both during setup. They look like any other text, so you can send one with someone sitting right next to you.

Exit word

Get a reason to leave

Text it and Batman replies like a friend, then texts you an urgent "call me" so you have an excuse to leave.

  1. Robin: pineapple
  2. Batman: omg ok one sec
  3. Batman: Call me asap

Alert word

Get help without a scene

Text it and Batman quietly alerts your friend with your location. You get an innocent reply, nothing that gives it away.

  1. Robin: blueberry
  2. Batman: haha ok
  3. Meanwhile, Alfred gets a heads-up with your location.

"pineapple" and "blueberry" are examples. Choose words you would never text by accident.

Plain answers before you trust it

What Batman does, what it keeps, and where it stops.

  • Your friend says yes first

    Batman texts the friend you pick and asks. Nothing is set up until they agree.

  • Location only during the plan

    You share it for that window and nothing longer. It's deleted 24 hours after the plan ends.

  • It watches you, not them

    Batman never looks up, tracks or messages the person you're meeting.

  • Batman is not 911

    It never calls emergency services. If something is wrong, it tells your friend to call. If you're in danger right now, call 911 yourself.

  • It can fail

    A dead phone, no signal or an outage can stop a check-in from getting through. Think of Batman as a backup, not a guarantee.

  • Adults only

    Batman is for people 18 and older.
